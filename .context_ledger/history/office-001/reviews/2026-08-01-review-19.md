### Chat layout containment

- Replaced the unconstrained end-marker `scrollIntoView()` call with an
  explicit `scrollTo()` on the transcript container, preventing outer document
  ancestors from being scrolled when a message/streaming placeholder appears.
- Added `min-h-0` to the CenterPanel root, center-column wrapper, and transcript
  flex child so the bounded workspace can shrink without pushing the header or
  composer out of position.
