## Executive Summary

`ActionHistory` — the left-panel action trace, the twin of the chat
Actions timeline (S210) — was already well-structured (numbered chains
grouped under plan steps) but **had no motion and no running-step
emphasis**: the "old" feel the user described. Applied the shared motion
vocabulary:

- steps animate in as they stream (framer-motion `AnimatePresence`);
- the running step gets an amber card ring + a pulsing numbered badge;
  done/failed badges pick up green/red so the numbered chain reads its
  own status at a glance;
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured.
