## Bearing on B-2026-08-18-1

- **(a) freeze/hide** and **(b) remove** both assume the multi-agent layer is a
  liability to the spec. It is not — the §40 criteria are satisfied by the
  **parent loop**, which stands with or without delegation. Removing it costs a
  large, risky diff and buys no §40 improvement.
- **(c) accept-and-harden** matches the evidence: keep the runtime, treat the
  spec as the quality bar, and close the 4 PARTIALs — in priority order:
  1. **Efficiency** (B-2026-08-17-8..12) — the one criterion that is materially
     behind; also the biggest user-visible latency/cost win.
  2. **Tool selection** L2-secondary (stop pre-create exploratory shell).
  3. **Recovery** — fix `classify_failure` terminal-only flagging (review-42).
  4. **Observation** — read-blocked false grounding (B-2026-08-17-7).
- If (c) is chosen, the one honesty debt is **the spec text itself**: §36/§43
  say specialists/delegation are out of scope, but they ship. Update the spec to
  record that LocalMind consciously advanced past the single-agent phase, and
  keep §40 as the standing bar.
